Drip irrigation cut water use by a third across 14 test farms

By A. Rivera, October 28, 2026

Across 14 test plots in California's Central Valley, drip irrigation cut water use by 31 percent compared with sprinklers, while yields stayed within 2 percent of the control group.

The largest gains came from clay soils, where evaporation losses were highest. Sandy plots saved 19 percent.

What it cost

Installation averaged $1,850 per acre. At current water prices, farmers recovered the cost in about 3 growing seasons.
